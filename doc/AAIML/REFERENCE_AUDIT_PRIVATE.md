# PRIVATE Reference Audit - DO NOT SUBMIT

**Manuscript:** *Graph-Grounded Template Generation for Layered Instruction Fine-Tuning of Small Language Models in Cyber Threat Intelligence*  
**Purpose:** private author-side verification of every citation in the double-blind AAIML submission. This file intentionally reveals identities and URLs that are masked in the review manuscript. **Do not include this file in the conference submission package.**

## Audit method

The conference bibliography was rebuilt as a cited-only subset of the uploaded long-form `glaukopis_cs_v1.5.bbl`. The two identity-bearing entries (AthenaBench and the Glaukopis repository) were replaced by anonymized review entries. Every cited work was then checked against a publisher/proceedings page, an official project/repository, an arXiv record, or another primary source. In addition to bibliographic existence, the sentence/table cell in which each citation is used was checked against what the source actually says.

**Result:** all 26 cited sources correspond to real works/artifacts, and the current manuscript's citation contexts are supportable. Two items deserve explicit nuance: (1) Qwen2.5 was first posted to arXiv in December 2024, while the canonical citation used by Qwen and the supplied long-form bibliography gives 2025; the manuscript keeps 2025. (2) *Toward Cybersecurity-Expert Small Language Models* was an arXiv preprint in 2025 and was subsequently published at ICML 2026; the review bibliography keeps the compact 2025 arXiv form from the supplied bibliography to preserve the 10-page limit. This is not a false citation, but the camera-ready bibliography can be upgraded to the ICML 2026 PMLR record.

## Cross-check against the uploaded long-form `.bbl`

The uploaded `glaukopis_cs_v1.5.bbl` contains 30 references. The conference paper cites 26 references. The conference subset is derived from the long-form references with the following intentional transformations:

- **AthenaBench** -> `anonbench` (identity/authors/title/DOI withheld in review copy).
- **Glaukopis software repository** -> `anonartifact` (repository owner/name/URL/commit withheld in review copy).
- Long-form-only **Minerva**, **CVE Program**, **CAPEC**, and **CWE** entries are not cited in the condensed conference manuscript and are therefore omitted from the conference BBL.
- Citation keys were normalized for the conference source (for example `alam2024ctibench` -> `ctibench`, `brown2020gpt3` -> `brown2020language`); this has no bibliographic effect.
- The MITRE ATT&CK report is represented with the official revised report metadata, `MP180360R1`, 2020 (originally published 2018, revised March 2020).
- The conference BBL is generated from the included cited-only `.bib` using `IEEEtran.bst`. The `.bbl` is included so the manuscript can compile without running BibTeX.

## Double-blind note

The submitted bibliography intentionally masks the two self-identifying references above. **Ariadne and Sophia remain named in the manuscript at the authors' request.** Those names are distinctive enough that a determined reviewer could potentially search for them, so retaining them carries some deanonymization risk even though author names, affiliations, repository owner/name/URL, Benchmark A identity, funding information, ORCID, and model-hosting URLs are removed.
