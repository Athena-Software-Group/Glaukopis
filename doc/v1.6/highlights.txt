Glaukopis: Auditable Graph-Grounded Instruction Tuning for Cyber Threat Intelligence

Peter J. Worth Jr., Ionut Cardei, Salman Ahmad, Muhammad Mamoon Khan

- Every CTI training target is the rendering of a typed query over a 12-source graph
- Retained checkpoints locate where specialization gains and loses capability
- Aligned benchmark 65.0 vs 57.7 base; external benchmarks and MMLU-Pro do not gain
- One 17,320-row structured-answer stage collapses attribution and skews CVSS
- Single-stage run performs comparably; no advantage is claimed for the schedule
