## What We Build

The Athena platform unifies SIEM, EDR, NDR, and XDR into a single AI-augmented operations layer:

- **Athena Core** — Unified SIEM/EDR/XDR foundation that centralizes telemetry across endpoints, cloud workloads, and critical infrastructure. Powered by Wazuh and extended by Athena engineering.
- **Athena NIDS** — Suricata-powered network intrusion detection with deep packet visibility across north-south and east-west traffic, including lateral movement and cloud network anomalies.
- **Athena AI Analyst (Pallas)** — Our proprietary AI analyst layer for alert triage, incident summarization, natural-language querying of security data, and action-oriented recommendations grounded in live telemetry.
- **Athena XDR+ (Ageleia)** — Aggregation and orchestration layer that applies contextual scoring and correlation to transform high-confidence detections into coordinated, multi-layer response.

## Why We're on Hugging Face

This organization is the home of **Athena Labs**, our applied research arm working at the intersection of AI and cyber defense. Our focus areas include:

- **Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) LLMs** — Evaluating, fine-tuning, and training open-source language models for core analyst workloads: adversary tactics extraction, threat-actor attribution, report classification and mapping, risk and mitigation scoring, and vulnerability and software profiling.
- **AthenaBench** — Our evaluation suite for measuring frontier and open-source LLM performance on real-world CTI tasks. Latest results are published at [athenasecuritygroup.ai/athena-labs/athenabench](https://athenasecuritygroup.ai/athena-labs/athenabench/).
- **Reinforcement Learning for Security** — Applied RLVR-style training methodologies on mid-scale open-source models, with systematic, ablation-driven comparisons across model families and parameter scales.
- **Production AI for SecOps** — The research and tooling that underpins Pallas and the broader Athena platform.

We share models, datasets, and evaluation artifacts here as part of our commitment to advancing the open-source security community.
